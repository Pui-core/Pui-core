# 生成素材の記録

組み込み `image_gen` を使用。CLI/APIへの切替なし。元の立ち絵と透過素材を両方保存し、背景・表情や造形の正確な一致は人間の最終判断と区別する。生成素材の著作者・本人の声であるといった誤認を招く表示は加えない。

## 透過立ち絵

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent full-body character cutout for a vertical TikTok motion graphic. Primary request: remove ONLY the plain white background from the provided illustration and return the SAME exact illustrated character with true transparent alpha. Preserve the character's exact facial identity, eyes, mint blue twin buns and braided hair, outfit, body proportions, pose, hands, shoes, colors and existing linework. Preserve delicate flyaway hair. Keep the entire original character head to shoes, with a small clear margin. Do not add shadows, objects, lettering, logos or a new background. Do not redesign, beautify, stylize, change expression, or crop any body part. Image 1 is the source illustration.

## ステージ背景

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: background plate for a high-end vertical 9:16 TikTok promotional motion graphic for a cute virtual livestreamer's bingo event. Create a tasteful anime-inspired 3D mixed-media stage set: deep charcoal indigo background, frosted mint glass arcs near the left and right edges, soft lilac rim light, a few tiny cherry-blossom petals and iridescent glints at the far corners, subtle depth and atmospheric blur. The middle 70 percent must be very calm dark negative space for large white typography and a bingo board to be added later. Small glossy mint and pale pink bingo balls arranged at the very bottom edge, no visible numbers. Stylish, collectible toy materials, soft studio lighting, premium Japanese kawaii music-streamer aesthetic, strong legibility, not busy, not a generic sci-fi interface. Full bleed vertical composition. No people, characters, text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark.
